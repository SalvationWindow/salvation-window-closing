38 Year Smoking Addiction Gone!

Submitted by TomR, Indiana

I was a two pack a day smoker for 38 years. I tried everything to quit: patches, nicotine gum,hypnosis,...you name it; nothing worked. On Sundays, after the Adult Sunday School class across the street from the church, I always slipped behind the church van to have a smoke before going into the church.This particular Sunday my mind must have wandered while smoking and I inadvertently stepped out from behind the van while puffing away. Well, I looked up and saw four kids from the Youth Group standing in the doorway of the church watching me smoke.

All of that day and even the next morning I felt so ashamed. Now if one of those kids started smoking I felt it would be my fault. I tried reading my Bible like I do every morning but couldn't concentrate. I finally closed it and told God I was sorry for not being able to quit that dirty little habit that was wrecking my health and badly influencing those kids. I confessed I was powerless to quit on my own and admitted that if I were to ever quit it would have to be His doing because I just couldn't do it.

That was August 15, 2008. I haven't had a cigarette since. He took away all of the cravings and it was if I had never smoked a day in my life. I learned that day that the only way to overcome addiction is by admitting I was powerless over it and giving it all to Him. Paise God for setting me free!
